### 10\. SEO page plan for launch

Do not create a separate URL for every combination of locality × configuration × property type immediately. That would generate hundreds of weak pages.

Publish in this order:

* Main city pages: Independent Floors in Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad and Ghaziabad.

* Priority Delhi locality pages: Greater Kailash I, Greater Kailash II, Defence Colony, Vasant Vihar, Anand Niketan, Niti Bagh, Anand Lok, Panchsheel Park, Hauz Khas, Green Park, South Extension, East of Kailash, Kailash Colony, Maharani Bagh, New Friends Colony, Jor Bagh and Golf Links.

* Major wider-market pages: Dwarka, Janakpuri, Punjabi Bagh, Paschim Vihar, Rajouri Garden, Rohini, Pitampura, Indirapuram, Vaishali, DLF Phase I–V and Sushant Lok.

* Configuration pages: 2 BHK, 3 BHK and 4 BHK independent floors in the strongest local markets first; add 1 BHK, 5 BHK+, duplex, triplex and penthouse pages where listings justify them.

* Transaction pages: Buy, sell and rent pages where each page provides a genuinely different user journey.
